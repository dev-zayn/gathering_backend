How it works

Four things happen between a tap on your tile and your first frame. Only the last one is yours to write.

sports_esportsA player taps your tileIn the app's store, on their shelf, or from a room's lobby.
lockThe app opens a sessionPOST /v1/games/{id}/session returns your entry URL with a one-time code.
rocket_launchYour page loadsFull screen in a WebView: entry?code=cod_…
hubYou talk to the bridgewindow.gathering: dialogs, storage, players, the room.
dnsYour page never opens a socket and never holds a token. A server of your own is optional; you need one only for your own accounts, leaderboards or anti-cheat, and it trades the ?code= for a game token (Your server).

One page load is one round.

Quickstart 5 steps

A turn-based game for 2–8 players, from an empty folder to a page the app can list. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step.

1

Lay out the files

Three files and the manifest, all on one https origin that loads without a login.

The page is right to left and dark, padded for the notch and the home indicator. The room keys stay hidden until practice mode shows them.

2

Wait for the bridge

The app injects window.gathering after your scripts start.

Never decide "I'm not in the app" because window.gathering is missing at load time. Copy bridgeReady() as it is: it resolves the bridge once the app installs it, or null in a plain browser.

Then start. Read the player, ask for the room, and hide the splash on every path, failures included.

In a desktop browser bridgeReady() gives null, and this stand-in keeps the game playable in practice mode. It is the only place alert and confirm may appear.

3

Make it playable alone

Pure rules first, so practice and the host share them.

applyMove returns a new state, or null when the move isn't allowed. render draws the whole screen from a state, every time.

Outside a room the whole game plays on one phone, and two keys offer a room for your game. create() and join() resolve { id, code }, then the room's lobby takes your page's place.

4

Play it in a room

The host referees; every phone draws from the shared state.

Other players send their moves as messages. The host checks each with applyMove and writes the state with the version it last saw. A state_stale rejection means someone wrote first: read the state again, then decide. When the round is won the host ends it with scores that rank every player (scoresFrom): a tournament counts each round by its ranks.

A real-time game ("host": "peer", "pace": "realtime") works differently: every phone counts from the round's startedAt, each input is one send, and the host ends the round with the scores it counted. It takes the place of the enterRoom above.

5

Publish the manifest

The only thing Gathering knows about your game.

Serve it at /.well-known/gathering-game.json, public (a private or localhost address is refused), as application/json, under 256 KB, answering within ten seconds. A catch-all route that returns your index.html is the most common mistake.

Check it from outside your network before you send it in:

The manifest, field by field

Validation reports every problem at once, as { "error": "invalid_fields", "fields": { … } }.

Page rules

Your page is the top document of a full-screen WebView: WKWebView on iOS, WebView on Android.

smartphonePad for the safe area

viewport-fit=cover, then env(safe-area-inset-top) and -bottom on your screen. With the bar hidden an iPhone draws your page under the status bar; on Android the app stops it there itself. The same CSS is right on both.

translateArabic first

<html lang="ar" dir="rtl"> and logical properties like margin-inline-start. getAppInfo().language if you ship English too.

dark_modeDark first

The app is dark by default. getDeviceInfo().isDarkMode tells you the app's look, which the player picks in the app, not the phone's setting.

web_assetThe bar is the app's

Your tile and name, the room's code and the app's controls. Nothing you draw covers it. Hide it with hideAppBar() and keep your own way out that calls g.close().

blockNo native dialogs

alert, confirm and prompt can block the WebView. Use showDialog and prompt from the bridge.

fit_screenOne screen, light to load

The frame is already full screen. Pinch-zoom and text selection start off. Don't load heavy assets before your first screen.

Use the bridge, not the browser

What your game does today, and the call that does it on Gathering. Every one is on g, the bridge bridgeReady() gave you.

A rejected promise rejects with a bare code string, never an Error. Put every code a player can meet into your own words.

Rooms

A room is a private table of 1–64 players with one host, and a relay that carries every message in the same order to every phone. The app owns the connection, the codes, invites, nearby rooms on the same Wi-Fi, reconnects and host hand-over. Remove your own lobby, matchmaking and sockets.

CallReturns and rules
room.current(){ id, code, game, status, round, you: { role, seat }, config, seq, startedAt, tournament } or null. status is lobby, playing or ended; role is host, player or spectator; tournament is null, or the tournament match the room plays.
room.members()[{ id, nickname, handle, avatar, avatarUrl, role, seat, connected }], by seat.
room.send(data, { to? })Any JSON under 16 KiB, at most 30 frames a second per player, state writes included. The relay drops what goes over, without a word. A message with to is not echoed to you, and one to someone not in the room is dropped.
room.getState(){ version, data }, with "state": "blob". Every round starts at version 0.
room.setState(data, version)The new version. Pass the one you last saw. Host-only with authoritative; no_state in a room with "state": "none".
room.start() / room.end({ scores })Host only. end ends the round and records its results; the room stays open. It rejects room_not_started when no round is playing (the platform may have ended it) and invalid_fields for a line naming nobody in the room, a duplicate, or a rank below 1.
room.invite()The app's invite sheet over your game; resolves the ids invited.
room.create() / room.join()Outside a room only. { id, code }, then the lobby takes your page's place.
room.leave() / room.kick(id)Leave for the app's hub / the host removes a player (tournament_room in a match room).
room.ticket(){ token, expiresAt }, a short-lived ES256 JWT for your server.

Events

g.room.on(event, fn) and g.room.off(event, fn?).

EventDetail
state{ version, data, by, seq }; after a reconnect it may be { version, data } alone. Render from this on every phone, the host's too.
message{ from, seq, data, to? }. Your own messages may come back: skip from === me.id.
membersThe member list (accept a bare array or { list }).
joined / left{ player, seq } with the whole member / { player, seq, reason } with the id. A dropped connection is not a leave: members shows it as connected: false.
started / endedended fires once per round with its results and a reason: host, timeout (the platform ends a round after two hours), empty, idle, admin, tournament, or empty for a plain end. Never score by it.
host{ seq, player, by }: the host changed. Check whether you are the host now.
kicked, connection{ seq, player, by } / { state }: connecting, open, reconnecting or closed, for a "reconnecting…" line.
emoji_events
Results

The host sends one entry per player that ranks them: g.room.end({ scores: [{ player: 'ply_1', score: 37, rank: 1 }, …] }). Leave out rank and the platform ranks by score, highest first, ties sharing a place (1, 1, 3). Each phone calls celebrate() for its own outcome; the app then shows the standings in the room's lobby.

emoji_events
Tournaments

A room may be a tournament's match room (room.current().tournament is not null). Your game needs nothing new: every round that ends with results is counted by its ranks. In a knockout the round's winner is the player ranked strictly better; equal ranks are a draw and the pair plays again, so a knockout needs room.players.min of 2 or less. Only the match's players get in (not_in_match), nobody is kicked or invited (room.kick and friends.invite reject tournament_room; the room.invite() sheet's invites are refused), and the deciding round ends with reason: 'tournament', after which the app shows its results with the way to the tournament. From your first screen, g.tournament.create() and g.tournament.join() start or join one; check that g.tournament exists first. The tournaments page has the rest.

Your own server optional

Skip this unless you keep your own accounts, leaderboards or anti-cheat. At registration you get a client_secret, shown once: keep it in your server's environment, never in the page.

The frame loads entry?code=cod_…. The code is good for one minute, one use. Send it from the page to your server:

replayRenew, don't loop

Renew with grant_type=refresh_token: the renewed token is live: false and carries no room. invalid_grant on renewal means the game was removed or suspended: stop. Names and pictures come only with identity:read.

lockRoom tickets

g.room.ticket() gives a JWT with the player, room, role, seat and round. Verify the signature against /.well-known/jwks.json, then the issuer, the audience (a list with your game id), the expiry (ten minutes) and that kind is room: only kind tells a ticket from your game token.

emoji_eventsResults

With results:write: POST /v1/bridge/results replaces that round's results whole, and a later host room.end leaves them as they are. Posted before the round ends, they count for a tournament match.

boltNotifications

With notify:send: POST /v1/bridge/notifications, a title up to 60 characters, a body up to 160, a deep_link; also pushed to the phone; 10 a player per game an hour. An idempotency_key keeps one per thing; a 401 means stop for that player.

Getting listed

  1. Publish the manifest

    And check it from outside your network: 200 application/json.

  2. Send your origin to your Gathering contact

    An admin registers it; the manifest is fetched and validated then. You get every error at once, or your client_secret.

  3. Gathering writes the store copy

    Tagline, icon, description, screenshots and a category: speed, brain, words, luck, guess, draw, party or other.

  4. An admin publishes it

    Until then the game answers 404 to players. New version, entry and name apply at once; wider scopes, changed permissions or a changed room block wait for an admin.
