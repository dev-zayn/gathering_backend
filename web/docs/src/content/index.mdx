## What Gathering is

Gathering (گعدة) is an app where friends open a **private room** and play a small game together,
online or sitting next to each other on the same Wi-Fi. A game is an HTML page you host. The app
loads it in a frame, puts the room's players in front of it, relays every message between them,
and keeps a shared state if the game wants one. Your page never opens a socket and never holds a
token: `window.gathering` is the whole of what it needs, and nothing about the player crosses the
frame except what [the bridge](/bridge) answers.

## What you build

- A page at the `entry` address in your [manifest](/manifest), laid out for a phone, Arabic first.
- Game logic on top of [`window.gathering.room`](/bridge#the-room): messages, members, a versioned
  state, one round at a time.
- Optionally, a server of your own: it verifies players through a [room ticket](/rooms#the-ticket),
  posts a round's [results](/rooms#results), and tells players things through their
  [inbox](/notifications).
